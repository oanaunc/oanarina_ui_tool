# Canvas cropping, nested frame fitting and hanging quotation marks

This continues the [responsive/type/vector pass](UNRELEASED-RESPONSIVE-OPENTYPE-SEPARATION.md). Three workflows gain working source implementations or broader coverage while remaining explicitly **partial**. The register records **1,245 implemented, 189 partial and 588 planned requirements**, out of 2,022. Exact Figma reference acceptance remains separate; Forma is still in development toward parity.

Review identified and corrected crop limit gestures that could detach a paint style while leaving paints unchanged. Reaching scale/pan bounds now preserves links and history when the operation has no effective change. Crop previews intentionally affect the selected source; normal component synchronization updates linked instances on release. Frame actions validate both the selected bounds choice and their live originating editor state. Paragraph quotation controls invalidate retained actions even through same-event mode/Motion away-and-back transitions.

Visual QA also exposed an inspector focus bug at 1280 × 720: an absolutely positioned hidden legend escaped its scroll container and let centered focus scroll the app header away. Positioning its own fieldset contains that label. At 125% UI scale, the Layers panel now scrolls locally and retains a minimum tree viewport, keeping footer, filters, section links and keyboard navigation reachable. Focus/scroll/preview regressions verify a fixed app header without changing artwork, history or camera.

`hangingQuotes` is optional, validated at layer/paragraph/style and transfer boundaries, and compatible with `.oui` versions one and two without a schema migration. The other workflows use existing geometry and paint fields. No native resource/IPC permissions or proprietary font distribution is added.
